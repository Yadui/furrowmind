Interview a veteran farmer

Gemini runs the interview. It asks one probing question at a time to surface the cues, thresholds and exceptions that never made it into a textbook. Farmers can type or speak, in Japanese or English.

Judges, quick path: press Start, then press Use sample answer and Send 3–4 times. Press Extract, then verify a record in tab 2 and download the dataset in tab 3. The sample farmer is simulated. Each interview turn takes about 3–6 s; Extract takes about 20–60 s.

Knowledge coverage

Coverage is Gemini's own estimate of how fully each part of a decision rule has been captured.

Verification queue

Every record shows what a generic AI said compared with the expert. A Gemini judge scores the knowledge gap and flags claims that conflict with mainstream guidance. A human reviewer (an extension officer or a peer farmer) then approves, edits or rejects the record. Only verified records go into the dataset.

SFT: expert answers for fine-tuning. DPO: expert answer (chosen) vs generic AI answer (rejected). Eval: held-out prompts with rubrics built from the insights the generic AI missed.
